## What is DoseCheck?

**DoseCheck** is a pharmacist-focused medication safety decision-support project being developed to explore how pharmaceutical knowledge, structured medication information, software engineering, natural language processing, and retrieval-based AI can work together to support safer medication review.

The system is being designed to explore potential identification of:

- 💊 Drug-drug interactions
- ⚠️ Contraindications
- 📏 Dose-related concerns
- 🧑‍⚕️ Patient-specific medication risks
- 📄 Prescription information
- 🔎 Relevant medication evidence
- 📝 Explainable medication-safety information

The objective is **not to replace pharmacists or doctors**.

The objective is to explore how software could help healthcare professionals **find, organise, interpret, and review relevant medication information more efficiently**.
